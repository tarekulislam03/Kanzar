Create a **cinematic motion-designed invitation website** for the grand opening of a luxury jewellery brand called **KANZAR JEWELS**.

The website should feel like a **professionally motion-designed luxury jewellery advertisement / invitation video**, not a normal website.

The entire experience should play like a **30–45 second cinematic film**. Each section should behave like a video scene, with carefully choreographed transitions, camera movement, typography animation, light effects, particles, logo animation and sound-design-ready timing.

### CORE EXPERIENCE

Use a full-screen 16:9-style cinematic composition adapted responsively for mobile.

The visitor should feel like they are watching a luxury jewellery commercial.

Do NOT make it look like:

* A normal website
* A SaaS landing page
* A wedding invitation template
* A collection of cards
* A conventional scrolling website

Instead, create a **continuous visual sequence** where one scene morphs into the next.

Scrolling should control the progression of the film, or optionally autoplay the sequence after the first interaction.

### SCENE 01 — THE HOOK

Black screen.

Almost complete silence visually.

A very subtle golden glow slowly appears from darkness.

Text fades in extremely slowly:

**“Did you know…”**

Hold for a moment.

The text disappears.

A cinematic light sweep crosses the screen.

Then reveal:

**“Something beautiful
is coming to Kolkata.”**

Use dramatic typography timing rather than simply displaying the text.

### SCENE 02 — BUILD ANTICIPATION

The previous text dissolves into darkness.

Tiny golden particles begin moving through the frame.

A soft golden light gradually expands.

Reveal:

**“A new chapter
in jewellery
is about to begin.”**

Animate each line independently with elegant motion.

The camera should feel as if it is slowly moving forward through darkness.

### SCENE 03 — BRAND REVEAL

Everything fades to black.

A thin golden light passes horizontally across the screen.

As the light passes, the **KANZAR JEWELS logo** begins appearing from darkness.

Create a premium logo reveal:

* Metallic gold
* Subtle shine
* Light reflection traveling across the logo
* Very slight particle dispersion
* Elegant scale/movement
* No flashy effects

First show:

**COMING SOON**

Then transition into:

**KANZAR JEWELS**

Give the logo enough screen time to feel like a real brand reveal.

### SCENE 04 — THE LOCATION

The logo slowly moves/fades away.

Transition using a cinematic golden light sweep.

Reveal:

**“Now opening in Kolkata”**

Then:

**[FULL LOCATION]**

Use a slow camera push-in toward an elegant architectural/jewellery visual.

If imagery is used, treat it like a cinematic advertisement shot rather than a website banner.

### SCENE 05 — THE INVITATION

Everything slows down.

Dark background.

A soft golden glow surrounds the typography.

Reveal:

**“We invite you
to witness the beginning.”**

Then dramatically reveal:

**GRAND OPENING**

Follow with:

**30 SEPTEMBER 2026**

The date should be treated as a major cinematic title card.

### SCENE 06 — THE OFFER REVEAL

Transition from the date using a bright golden light flash or elegant gold particle transition.

The screen becomes visually richer.

Reveal:

**GRAND OPENING OFFER**

Pause.

Then reveal the main offer in large typography:

**3.99%**

Then:

**MAKING CHARGES**

Then:

**ON 22K GOLD JEWELLERY**

Each element should appear sequentially like a motion-graphics commercial.

Supporting information appears afterward:

**9K & 18K GOLD JEWELLERY ALSO AVAILABLE**

Then:

**Offer valid until 6 October 2026**

The offer should feel exclusive and premium, not like a cheap promotional sale.

### SCENE 07 — FINAL BRAND SHOT

All elements slowly disappear.

Return to deep black.

A soft golden light illuminates the logo.

Show:

**KANZAR JEWELS**

Then:

**“A new story in gold begins here.”**

Finally:

**30 SEPTEMBER 2026**
**[FULL LOCATION], KOLKATA**

End with a subtle gold particle fade into black.

### MOTION DESIGN

The most important requirement is **motion design quality**.

Use:

* Cinematic scene transitions
* Scroll-controlled animation
* Smooth camera movement
* Slow zooms
* Parallax depth
* Mask reveals
* Text tracking/letter-spacing animation
* Character-by-character typography reveals where appropriate
* Golden light sweeps
* Volumetric glow
* Fine floating gold particles
* Elegant blur transitions
* Metallic reflections
* Scale and opacity choreography
* Morphing between scenes
* Carefully controlled easing
* Long, luxurious animation timing

Every animation should have a purpose.

Avoid:

* Bouncy animations
* Generic fade-ins everywhere
* Excessive particle effects
* Fast transitions
* Neon effects
* Overly bright gold
* Generic Framer Motion presets

The motion should resemble **high-end jewellery commercials, luxury fashion films and premium brand title sequences**.

### VISUAL LANGUAGE

Color palette:

* Near-black / charcoal
* Warm ivory
* Champagne gold
* Subtle metallic gold

Typography should combine:

**Elegant high-fashion serif** for cinematic statements

with

**minimal modern sans-serif** for supporting information.

Use huge typography and extremely generous negative space.

### WEBSITE BEHAVIOUR

Make the experience feel like a video even though it is implemented as a website.

Use:

**Next.js + React + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion**

Use scroll progress to drive the cinematic timeline.

Each scene should occupy approximately one full viewport, but the actual animation should progress continuously based on scroll position.

Add smooth scrolling.

On mobile, preserve the same cinematic storytelling rather than simply stacking website sections.

The website should be optimized for sharing through WhatsApp, with the experience working beautifully on a phone.

Design the implementation so that real jewellery photographs, the actual Kanzar Jewels logo and the exact store location can easily be inserted later.

The final result should feel like:

**“A luxury jewellery motion graphic film that happens to be an interactive website.”**
